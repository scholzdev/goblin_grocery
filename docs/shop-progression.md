# Shop progression and customer deals

Normal runs improve shop quality automatically with the completed aisle, without spending money. Milestones at aisles 1, 3, 5 and 7 retain the existing quality table. Star and legendary probabilities interpolate between these milestones on each aisle, capped at aisle 7 (3% to 16% legendary chance per slot). Legendaries remain unavailable in shop 1. Department bonuses and guaranteed legendary rewards still apply. Locked offers keep their existing tier and price.

From aisle 5 the shop also grants its own first free reroll and the third route option. Lab quality remains manually selectable.

A customer may visit once per shop: 25% in aisle 1, +5 percentage points per aisle, capped at 55%. A separate stream seeded by run seed and aisle chooses the visit and one eligible owned item. Rerolling offers never changes the customer. The offer costs the existing item upgrade price (base price at ★1, twice the base price at ★2) and adds exactly one star. Legendary and ★3 items are excluded, as are cart items whose upgraded shape cannot fit.

Accepting rechecks ownership, item type, original tier, price, wallet and available space before charging. Moving an item to the shelf is allowed. Selling or merging the target invalidates its offer. Buying and declining each close the deal for that visit; neither increases shop quality. The next shop has its own visit roll.
